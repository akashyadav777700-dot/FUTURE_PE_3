## 📝 1. The Magic Prompt 🪄
Here is the exact structured prompt I used to guide the AI for this task:

> **Role: You are a Senior SEO Content specialist and copywriter.

Context: Create a SEO content cluster strategy and 1 long-form blog for a local salon named "Hair & Care"

Task:
1) Pillar page concept: Define high intent pillar topic for salon's website, targeting main keywords and building trust and authority.(Example-  Best Salon in Delhi NCR- Pocket friendly price for young adults and students)

2) Content Cluster: Suggest 3-5 similar blogs that support the main topic of pillar page.

3) SEO Blog Structure: Write a complete SEO Blog including:
• H1-H3 Heading and sub-topics.
• Clear and relatable, Strong Call-To-Action (CTA) section.
• Internal linkage to service/booking page.
• Focus Keyword and local search traffic.

Format: Use markdown, bulletin point and bold text for easy reading. Friendly tone, easy to understand and engaging content.**
